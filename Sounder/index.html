A LITTLE ABOUT US

Made for the
sound seekers.

A-Soundscape searches Openverse, Internet Archive, and Wikimedia Commons together, and can also connect to Freesound and Jamendo when you add their free developer credentials in settings.json. Search by keyword, play available recordings, and open the original source page for details and rights information.

Results load in larger pages from each enabled catalog to keep browsing fast. Audio rights vary by item, so check each recording’s license and usage terms before reusing it.

LIVE CATALOG CONNECTION

Browse Open Sounds.

On GitHub Pages, A-Soundscape searches Openverse directly from your browser. To combine Internet Archive, Wikimedia Commons, Freesound, and Jamendo, deploy the Python backend separately. Catalogs have their own rate limits and licensing rules.
