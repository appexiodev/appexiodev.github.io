Privacy Policy

How AppExio Dev handles information when you use our websites.

1. Introduction

AppExio Dev respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our websites or use our independent digital products.

Our products are designed to provide useful digital tools while collecting as little personal information as reasonably possible.

2. Information We Collect

AppExio Dev does not require you to create an account simply to browse our developer website.

Depending on the specific product you use, information may be processed locally in your browser or device to provide the requested functionality.

If you contact us directly by email or another communication method, we may receive information that you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message, or other details.

3. How We Use Information

Information voluntarily provided to us may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Respond to feedback and suggestions.
  • Communicate regarding our products or services.
  • Improve our websites and products.
  • Maintain the security and functionality of our services.

4. Browser and Technical Information

Our websites may automatically receive limited technical information through standard web technologies, hosting infrastructure, or third-party services.

This information may include general information such as browser type, device type, operating system, approximate usage information, or technical request information.

This information is generally used for security, performance, troubleshooting, and improving the user experience.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Some AppExio Dev websites may use browser storage technologies such as local storage to remember preferences, including theme settings.

These technologies may store information directly in your browser and are not necessarily used to identify you personally.

6. Third-Party Services

Our websites may use or link to third-party services such as hosting providers, content delivery networks, analytics services, social media platforms, or external websites.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies. AppExio Dev does not control the privacy practices of external services.

7. Product-Specific Privacy

AppExio, A-PDF, and A-Cropper are independent products developed as separate projects under AppExio Dev.

Individual products may have different functionality and may therefore have additional privacy considerations. Where applicable, product-specific privacy information will be provided on the relevant website.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information that is provided to us. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our websites are not specifically directed toward collecting personal information from children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for unnecessary purposes.

10. External Links

Our websites may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of those external websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any third-party website you choose to visit.

11. Your Choices

You can choose not to provide personal information when it is not necessary for using a particular feature.

You can also control or clear browser storage and cookies through your browser settings.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our websites, products, or practices.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, please contact us through our Contact page.
